## Feeding
Adult cats are usually fed two or more measured meals a day. Measuring food helps prevent obesity, a common problem in indoor cats. Fresh, clean water should be available at all times; some cats drink more from a fountain.

## Litter box
Scoop the litter box at least once a day and change the litter regularly. A dirty box is a common reason cats stop using it.

## Play and enrichment
Indoor cats need daily play to stay fit and avoid boredom. Wand toys, puzzle feeders and scratching posts provide exercise and enrichment.

## Grooming
Brush your cat regularly, especially long-haired cats, to reduce hairballs and matting.

## Vet care
Adult cats should see a veterinarian at least once a year for a checkup and any vaccinations the vet recommends.
