## Feeding
Puppies grow fast and have small stomachs, so they eat several small meals a day. Most puppies do well on three to four meals a day until about six months old, then two to three meals a day. Feed a food labeled for growth or puppies. Fresh, clean water should be available at all times.

## Potty training
Puppies need frequent bathroom breaks: right after waking up, after every meal, after play, and before bed. Young puppies generally cannot hold it for long stretches, so a midday break is important if the owner is away during the day. A consistent schedule speeds up house training.

## Exercise and play
Puppies need several short play and exercise sessions spread through the day rather than one long, hard session. Short walks, gentle play and supervised exploration are better for growing joints than long runs.

## Training and socialization
Short, positive training sessions of a few minutes, once or twice a day, work best for puppies. Gentle exposure to new people, sounds and places helps them grow into confident adults.

## Vet care
Puppies need a series of vaccinations and deworming visits during their first months, scheduled by a veterinarian. Ask your vet for the exact schedule. Spaying or neutering should also be discussed with your vet.
