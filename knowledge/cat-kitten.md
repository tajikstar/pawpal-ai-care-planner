## Feeding
Kittens need food made for kittens and should eat several small meals a day; three to four meals a day is common for young kittens. Fresh, clean water should be available at all times.

## Litter box
Keep the litter box in a quiet, easy-to-reach spot and scoop it at least once a day. Kittens learn to use the box quickly when it is kept clean.

## Play and enrichment
Kittens have lots of energy. Several short play sessions a day with toys they can chase help them burn energy and learn not to play-bite hands. Scratching posts give them a place to scratch.

## Vet care
Kittens need a series of vaccinations and deworming visits in their first months, scheduled by a veterinarian. Ask your vet about the exact schedule and about spaying or neutering.
