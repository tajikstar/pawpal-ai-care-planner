## Health checks
Senior dogs should see a veterinarian more often than younger dogs; many vets recommend a checkup every six months. Older dogs are more likely to develop arthritis, dental disease, kidney disease and other conditions, and early detection helps.

## Exercise
Senior dogs still need daily activity, but shorter and gentler walks are often better than long, intense exercise. Watch for stiffness or limping and tell your vet about changes in mobility.

## Feeding
Some senior dogs benefit from food formulated for older dogs or for a specific health condition. Any special diet should be chosen with your veterinarian. Keep fresh water available at all times and note changes in thirst or appetite.

## Comfort
Soft bedding, non-slip floors and easy access to water help older dogs stay comfortable.
