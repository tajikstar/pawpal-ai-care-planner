## Feeding
Most adult dogs do well on two meals a day, about eight to twelve hours apart. Use a complete, balanced food suited to the dog's size and activity level, and avoid feeding lots of table scraps. Fresh, clean water should be available at all times.

## Exercise
Adult dogs need daily exercise to stay healthy and avoid boredom. Walks, play and games like fetch help. Most dogs benefit from at least two outings a day; high-energy breeds need more.

## Grooming and hygiene
Brush your dog regularly; how often depends on the coat. Brushing a dog's teeth several times a week, ideally daily, helps prevent dental disease. Check ears and trim nails as needed.

## Vet care
Adult dogs should see a veterinarian at least once a year for a checkup and any vaccinations the vet recommends. Ask your vet about heartworm, flea and tick prevention.
